Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each burn for a clean, even flame.

On the first burn, let the wax melt edge to edge (about 2–3 hours) so future burns stay even.

Keep the candle out of direct sunlight, drafts, and away from anything flammable.

Stop burning when about ½ inch of wax remains — then clean out the vessel and reuse it.

Premium coconut-soy wax blend, fine fragrance and essential oils, cotton wick. Vegan, phthalate-free, clean-burning. Hand-painted glass vessel.
